# Contributing to Master's Degree

This repository is a personal archive of coursework from my Master's program.
It is published so others can study from it, but it is not a collaborative
project in the usual sense: the material reflects assignments I submitted.

By participating you agree to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never through a public
issue.

## What is welcome

- **Errata.** If you find a mistake in a report, a derivation or a piece of
  code, open a [correction issue][correction]. Small fixes as pull requests are
  fine too.
- **Questions about the material** in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/vitorparras/Masters-Degree/discussions).
- **Reproducibility fixes**: missing dependency versions, broken paths, build
  instructions that no longer work.

[correction]: https://github.com/vitorparras/Masters-Degree/issues/new?template=content_error.yml

## What is not accepted

- New assignments, solutions to other courses or unrelated material.
- Rewrites that change the substance of a submitted assignment.

## Academic integrity

This material is for reference and learning. Do not submit it, in whole or in
part, as your own work. Check your institution's rules before reusing it.
